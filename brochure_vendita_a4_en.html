5 Key Strengths of the Home
  • 1. 9th and Top Floor (Views & Light):
    Above, there is only the large shared roof terrace with the solar panels: no neighbours upstairs. Traffic stays far below, muffled by the triple glazing.
  • 2. Private Covered Parking Space S1 (13 m²):
    An owned parking space, rare in this area. The lift goes from the 9th floor down to the entrance hall; from there, 2 flights of indoor stairs (14 steps) lead down to the garage.
  • 3. Class B & 110% Superbonus Works Already Done:
    External thermal insulation all around the home, triple-glazed windows, security door and condensing boiler (2022–2023). Already among the most efficient classes under the EU green homes directive; class B lets you apply for a green mortgage, on the bank's terms.
  • 4. Ramp Access & Everything on the Doorstep:
    No steps all the way to the flat: a ramp up to the entrance and a lift to the 9th floor. The “Il Giulia” shopping centre (PAM supermarket) is 30–40 metres from the door, almost all under cover (just one short open stretch); the stop for buses 6, 9 and 35, to the city centre, the station and the sea, is about 60 metres away.
  • 5. Legal Certainty:
    Conversion to full ownership already approved at €0 by the City of Trieste (notarial agreement pending); free sale price, with no PEEP restrictions; planning compliance certified by an architect.
An extra safeguard for the buyer: Trieste uses the land register system (Libro Fondiario): ownership passes on registration, which a judge checks beforehand. The flat will be vacant on completion.

Direct Private Sale: How to Make an Offer

The sale is handled directly with the owner, Marco Laurenti, with no agency: no commission for either seller or buyer.

In the written offer, just state:

  1. the amount offered;
  2. how you will pay: cash or an already approved mortgage;
  3. when you would like to complete.

Once the offer is accepted, the preliminary contract is signed with a deposit; completion then takes place at the office of the notary chosen by the buyer, in Trieste, who also checks the land register.
